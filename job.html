Ruby on Rails Developer (AI Assistance)

Full-timeRemote · work from anywhereMon–Fri, 9 AM–6 PM (WIB)Competitive pay

Are you a Ruby on Rails expert who builds faster with AI, and knows exactly when the AI is wrong? We want you.

We are hiring for a fintech client whose core platform runs on Ruby on Rails. You will work on a production system that moves real money: wallets, transfers, and transactions. Correctness matters more than speed.

Why join
  • Fully remote: work from anywhere.
  • Fixed working hours: Monday to Friday, 9 AM–6 PM WIB.
  • Good, competitive pay.
  • Real production Rails, with AI tools as part of your daily workflow.

What you will do

  • Build and maintain features in a mature Rails codebase.
  • Write and review code that handles money safely: transactions, locking, idempotency.
  • Use AI tools (Cursor, Claude Code, Copilot, or similar) to move fast, and review their output critically.
  • Write tests (RSpec) and fix production issues.
  • Help keep the codebase healthy: upgrades, performance, background jobs.

Must have

  • 4+ years of professional Ruby on Rails experience in production.
  • Solid PostgreSQL: indexes, transactions, locking, slow queries.
  • Background jobs (Sidekiq or similar) and automated testing (RSpec or Minitest).
  • Daily use of AI coding tools, and real examples of catching their mistakes.
  • Fluent English, spoken and written: the interviews and daily work are in English.

Nice to have

  • Fintech, payments, wallet, or ledger experience.
  • Rails version upgrades.
  • Open-source contributions or a public GitHub with real Rails work.

Our process (no long take-home)

  1. Short online screening form, 15–20 minutes.
  2. 10-minute English conversation with our HR team.
  3. 30-minute live call: a short no-AI code-reading check, then build a small feature with your own AI tools while you talk us through it.
  4. Technical interview with the client.
  5. Management interview.

We aim to reply at every stage within 5 working days.
